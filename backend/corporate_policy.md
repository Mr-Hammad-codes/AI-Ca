# AI-CA Enterprise Expense & Procurement Policy (India Region)
**Effective Date:** Current Fiscal Year
**Applies To:** All full-time employees and registered vendors operating in India.

## 1. General Procurement & Thresholds
* All single-invoice purchases exceeding ₹50,000 INR strictly require an approved Purchase Order (PO) prior to execution.
* Expedited shipping costs are not covered unless explicitly authorized by a VP or higher.

## 2. Tax & Vendor Compliance
* **GST Requirement:** Any invoice with a total value exceeding ₹5,000 INR must include a valid 15-character GSTIN (Goods and Services Tax Identification Number) from the vendor. Invoices without a GSTIN will be flagged as non-compliant.
* **TDS Applicability:** Professional and technical services are subject to Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) under section 194J. Vendors must clearly state their PAN (Permanent Account Number) if service fees exceed ₹30,000 INR.

## 3. IT Software & Cloud Services
* Recurring SaaS or cloud infrastructure subscriptions (AWS, Azure, GCP) exceeding ₹25,000 INR per month require explicit clearance from the regional IT Director.
* Software licenses must be billed annually, not monthly, to optimize forex and processing fees.

## 4. Hardware Procurement
* Enterprise hardware (laptops, monitors, servers) must be procured strictly from authorized national distributors or direct OEMs (e.g., Dell India, Apple India, HP India, Reliance Digital).
* Peripheral purchases (keyboards, mice, cables) are capped at a maximum of ₹3,500 INR per employee per year. 

## 5. Travel & Entertainment
* Domestic air travel is strictly limited to Economy class.
* Alcohol expenses are explicitly banned from corporate reimbursement or vendor invoices.
